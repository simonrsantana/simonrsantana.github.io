About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Quantitative Methods, Faculty of Economics, Business and Tourism (FEET), at the Universidad de Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (ULPGC). My Ph.D. in Mathematical Engineering, Statistics and Operations Research — on machine learning, statistics, and operations research, supervised by Daniel Hernández-Lobato — and my postdoctoral research at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (ICMAT-CSIC), with David Ríos Insua, form the core of how I work today. I was also a visiting scholar at Aalto University, Finland, working with Aki Vehtari.

My work sits at the intersection of Bayesian statistics and machine learning: I develop scalable approximate inference methods — variational inference, implicit processes, Laplace approximations — and apply them to problems in drug discovery and AI for biomedicine more broadly, adversarial risk analysis, and probabilistic approaches to AI.

I hold a degree in Physics from the Autonomous University of Madrid (UAM) and a Master's in Theoretical Physics from Complutense University of Madrid (UCM).
