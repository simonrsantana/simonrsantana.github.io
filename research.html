Research

My work sits at the intersection of Bayesian statistics and machine learning: I develop scalable approximate inference methods — variational inference, implicit processes, Laplace approximations — and apply them to problems in drug discovery and AI for biomedicine more broadly, adversarial risk analysis, and probabilistic approaches to AI.
